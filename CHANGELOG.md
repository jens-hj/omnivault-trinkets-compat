# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- NeoForge support, in its own jar.
- Curios support on NeoForge: wear your backpack in the Curios back slot.
- Trinkets support now also works on NeoForge.
- Accessories support is planned once Accessories is released for Minecraft 26.3.

## 0.1.0
- Initial release: OmniVault backpacks can be worn in the Trinkets back slot. They show on your back, open with the backpack key and keep their upgrades running.
